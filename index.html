ABOUT FIRST PRIORITY TRADING LLC

Your Automotive Spare Parts Partner

First Priority Auto Spare Parts Trading LLC was launched and licensed in 2007 and is based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

We are the exclusive distributor of MICHIBA automotive lighting bulbs and Veethree automotive gauges, as well as the manufacturer of FP aftermarket LED lights.

We support automotive workshops, transportation companies, automotive retailers and other business customers with product solutions and enquiries.
