Our Story

Who we are

First Priority Auto Spare Parts Trading LLC

Established in 2007, First Priority Auto Spare Parts Trading L.L.C. is a Dubai-based automotive spare parts company committed to delivering quality, reliability, and value to the automotive industry. Strategically located in the heart of Dubai, United Arab Emirates, we have built a strong presence in the wholesale and retail automotive electrical spare parts market.

We are the exclusive distributor of Michiba Automotive Lighting Bulbs, Epina Denki, Veetree Automotive Gauges, EXP, as well as the manufacturer of FP Products. Through our diverse product portfolio, we cater to the evolving needs of the automotive aftermarket, offering dependable solutions that meet industry standards.

Over the years, we have proudly served a diverse customer base, including automotive workshops, transportation companies, automotive retailers, and semi-government organizations across the Middle-east region as well as African region. Our customers remain at the heart of everything we do, inspiring us to continuously improve our products, services, and commitment to excellence.

At First Priority, we believe that quality should go hand in hand with affordability. Our extensive wholesale and retail operations enable us to offer competitively priced products without compromising on quality or performance.

To ensure a reliable and diverse product range, we strategically source and import automotive electrical spare parts from Taiwan, Turkey, China, Thailand, and India. By working with international suppliers and maintaining a strong focus on product quality, we strive to meet the varying requirements of our customers.
